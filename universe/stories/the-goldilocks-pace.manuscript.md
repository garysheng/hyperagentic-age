# The Goldilocks Pace

## Spread 1

On the other side of the portal is open ground. Every founder who makes it through arrives at the same place: a green plain with room to build, and a machine waiting with their name on it. The machine is an engine that can run at any speed. Any speed at all.

## Spread 2

The engines are identical. The plots are identical. On day one, the only thing that separates one founder from another is the dial on top of the engine. Nobody tells you where to set it.

## Spread 3

One founder pushes his dial as far as it goes. The engine roars. Towers rise overnight, whole floors at a time. From a distance it looks like winning.

## Spread 4

Up close, the floors are hollow. Doors open onto air. Stairs end halfway. He ships whatever the engine produces, because his neighbors are loud and he is afraid of falling behind.

## Spread 5

The people who moved in move out. They leave the way trust leaves: quietly, no slam, just a door that does not open again. A floor can be rebuilt in a night. A tenant cannot.

## Spread 6

Another founder barely starts her engine. She circles it. She reads about it. She waits for it to be safer, cheaper, more proven. The plain around her fills with lights, and her plot stays dark, and the era does not wait with her.

## Spread 7

Too hot burns the trust down. Too cold never builds it. Somewhere between the roar and the silence there is a third setting. The old story had it right: you are looking for just right.

## Spread 8

Maya walks her plot before she builds anything. Then she digs. To carry a tall building, the ground has to be opened deep first. The tallest towers on the plain start as the deepest holes.

## Spread 9

There are two ways to lay a subway under a young city. Tokyo laid lines it could extend, and extends them still. New York laid lines it can barely repair. What you bury badly, you live over forever. Maya lays the kind you can extend.

## Spread 10

Then she sets the dial. She picks the fastest speed her foundations can carry, and she leaves it there. The engine hums. It hums in week nine the way it hummed in week one. Steady is a speed too.

## Spread 11

Every season, the keepers send down a better engine. Maya's first job on delivery day is deletion. Half her scaffolding existed to steady the old engine, and the new one does not need it. Guardrails are built for the machine you had. She takes them down on purpose, the day the new machine arrives.

## Spread 12

A year passes. The tower of the founder who sprinted stands wrapped in repairs he can no longer afford. The plot of the founder who waited is still dark. And Maya's tower is rising faster than either of them ever moved, floor after solid floor.

## Spread 13

Visitors ask Maya where she got her engine. She shows them: the same engine everyone has. What they are looking for is the dial she can defend, the deep hole under her ground floor, and the deletion day she keeps every season. Her secret is a pace she can hold.

## Spread 14

The engines can go a million miles an hour. The founders who last go just right. Too hot burns. Too cold starves. Just right compounds.
