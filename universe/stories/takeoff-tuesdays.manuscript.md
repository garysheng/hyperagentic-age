# Takeoff Tuesdays

## Spread 1

There is a room in San Francisco where agentic startups learn to fly. It is not a stage and it is not a showroom. It is a good ordinary room with a plain door, and on Tuesdays the door is open. Come on Tuesday. Leave flying.

## Spread 2

Nobody famous guards the door. David holds it, with his hand already out, because a door is just an introduction between a hallway and a room.

## Spread 3

Inside, Gary's job is to name what the founders are looking at. The window that is open right now. The foundation they can rent instead of rebuild. The way up. He is the one who says out loud what a room full of builders is standing inside of.

## Spread 4

David's job is older than software. He notices who is standing alone at the edge of the room, crosses it with his hand already out, and says the four words he was born to say. Let me introduce you.

## Spread 5

Every Tuesday the room fills with founders. They do not come to watch a presentation. They bring what they built that week, and what broke, and what shocked them, and they put it on the table.

## Spread 6

A founder opens her laptop and the room leans in. Her agent ran all week while she slept. The question is never whether it worked. The question is what she learned when it did not.

## Spread 7

What one startup figures out, every startup gets. The practices are paved into playbooks, and the playbooks are handed to whoever walks in next. Nobody pays the figure-it-out cost twice.

## Spread 8

And the introductions compound. The storyteller meets the engineer she needed. The engineer meets the customer he could not find. David keeps the map of the room in his head, and he spends it on other people.

## Spread 9

Underneath every one of them hums the same foundation. Because they all build on the same infrastructure, help transfers. A fix found by one team on Tuesday is running inside four other startups by Friday.

## Spread 10

Then the first one lifts. A startup that walked in with a voice memo and a hunch leaves the ground, wings out, climbing. The room does not applaud politely. The room roars, because everyone in it knows exactly what that climb cost. Come on Tuesday. Leave flying.

## Spread 11

By the wall there is a shelf of small winged towers, one for every startup that took off from this room. Nobody dusts a trophy case here. The founders touch them on the way in, the way you touch a doorframe in a house you grew up in.

## Spread 12

The ones who flew keep coming back. They stand where they once sat, and they hand down what the climb taught them. In most industries the winners hoard altitude. In this room, altitude is the thing you owe the next batch.

## Spread 13

Some Tuesdays the founders of the lab itself slip in at the back to listen. The people building the intelligence want to hear what the room is doing with it, because the room finds uses the lab never imagined.

## Spread 14

The room outgrows its walls, so the door multiplies. The same plain leaf appears in Austin, in London, in Bangalore, in Tokyo. Different walls, one design, one promise. Come on Tuesday. Leave flying.

## Spread 15

Here is why it becomes the most valuable community in the world, and it is not the money. Every member's hardest-won lesson becomes every member's starting point. A thousand startups learning together compound faster than any company can learn alone.

## Spread 16

The world starts to notice that the agentic startups that win keep naming the same room. Investors ask for it. Rivals copy the furniture. But a community is not furniture. It is ten thousand introductions that already happened.

## Spread 17

Follow the community far enough and you can see where it is headed: a place where building the good future is a neighborhood activity. Warm, quiet, unsteered by any one hand, held up by everyone in it.

## Spread 18

And at the center, still, two people at a door. One names the room so builders know what they are standing inside of. One crosses it with his hand already out. Neither craft ever changed. The objects just got bigger.

## Spread 19

So bring the thing you are building. Bring the voice memo and the hunch. The door is plain on purpose, because the promise is not the door. The promise is the room behind it, and the room is expecting you.

## Spread 20

Come on Tuesday. Leave flying.
