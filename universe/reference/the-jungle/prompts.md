## establishing (1536x1024) -> reference/the-jungle/establishing.png
An EMPTY establishing plate of a vast emerald jungle interior, nobody in it. A narrow hand-cut trail, one person wide and marked by a few cut wooden stakes, winds away from the low camera into layered green depth. Giant broad leaves close overhead, cathedral-tall trees with buttress roots taller than a standing adult's shoulder, hanging lianas, ferns and moss. Warm shafts of morning daylight drop through the canopy and pool on the trail. The green stacks in soft layered washes, nearer leaves darker and crisper, far depth pale and warm. The mood is adventurous and alive, generous with light, never gloomy. Soft ink-and-wash illustration with visible brushwork and watercolor paper texture throughout. No people, no animals, no buildings, no text.
