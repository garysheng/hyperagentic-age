# the-steps: generation prompts

Register anchor (`reference/style/warm-editorial-neutral/refs/warm-editorial-swatch.png`) is passed FIRST on every shot. Warm editorial ink-and-wash; never neon, 3D/CGI/Pixar, glossy plastic, HUD/arc-reactor comic vocabulary, neo-comic.

REQUIRED before any render: `hero`. Shoot it first, then chain `detail` off it.

These bodies are the prompts that ACTUALLY produced the locked goldens, transcribed from their recipes. They were written at generation time rather than here, which is the wrong order: the entity's prompts belong in this file first so a re-shoot starts from the same words.

## hero  -> reference/the-steps/hero.png

Warm editorial storybook illustration, soft painterly line and gentle shading, muted natural palette
on a warm cream ground. Picture-book register for adults. NEVER comic, NEVER cartoon, NEVER flat
vector, NEVER photoreal, NEVER neon, NEVER 3D or CGI or Pixar, NEVER glossy plastic. Use the
reference ONLY for the illustration STYLE.
An ISOLATED OBJECT STUDY on a plain flat warm cream field, empty edge to edge, with soft shadows
beneath.
SUBJECT: a short flight of FOUR rising steps climbing from the lower left toward the upper right,
seen from the side. The flight is cut off by the top edge of the frame and clearly continues beyond
it.
The lowest two steps are solid, substantial, warm cream stone, gently worn and rounded at their
edges from long use. Resting on each of those two finished steps is one small settled object: a neat
little folded cloth on the first, a small closed pot on the second. They sit there comfortably, as
if they belong.
The third step is present but lighter, its edges softer and less certain, as though it is still
setting. The fourth is barely a suggestion, a pale outline dissolving upward into warm light before
it reaches the frame edge.
Nothing is broken, cracked, chipped, ruined, or missing. The upper steps are forming, not decaying.
Calm, warm, patient, quietly hopeful. No people, no figures, no hands, no ladder, no flag, no
banner, no summit, no mountain top, no doorway at the top, no prize. No landscape, no horizon, no
sky. No text, no letters, no numbers, no words anywhere.
READABLE SURFACES FACE THEIR READER: any book, page, letter, scroll or sign a character is reading
or writing faces THAT CHARACTER, never the camera. Compose over-the-shoulder or from behind so the
surface genuinely faces them. Any handwriting is abstract handwriting-like line work with NO real
readable letters.
NO USER INTERFACE ANYWHERE: no windows, menu bars, buttons, icons, toolbars, panels, form fields,
cursors, floating rectangles or screenshot-like elements. Screens carry only soft glow or vague
painterly shapes.

Invariants the read-back must check:
1. FOUR rising steps, seen from the side, and the flight continues up beyond the top edge of the frame. It never shows a summit, a top step, or an arrival
2. The two lowest steps are solid, warm and visibly WORN from use. Each higher step is fainter and less formed than the one below it, the topmost barely a suggestion
3. The finished steps CARRY something: small settled objects resting on them, so a completed step is visibly worth standing on by itself
4. Nothing is broken, cracked, or missing. The faint upper steps are BECOMING, never ruined
5. No figure climbing, no ladder, no flag, no summit marker, no prize, no goal at the top. The direction is the point and the destination is not shown
6. Warm editorial register: cream ground, soft painterly line, muted natural palette
7. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, or readable marks
