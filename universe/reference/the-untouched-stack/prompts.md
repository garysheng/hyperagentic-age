# the-untouched-stack: generation prompts

Register anchor (`reference/style/warm-editorial-neutral/refs/warm-editorial-swatch.png`) is passed FIRST on every shot. Warm editorial ink-and-wash; never neon, 3D/CGI/Pixar, glossy plastic, HUD/arc-reactor comic vocabulary, neo-comic.

REQUIRED before any render: `hero`. The `detail` slot is deliberately left null: the hero already carries the sheet edges, the bare top and the dust, and declaring a shot nobody intends to take is how an entity fails validate for no reason.

## hero  -> reference/the-untouched-stack/hero.png

Warm editorial storybook illustration, soft painterly line and gentle shading, muted natural palette
on a warm cream ground. Picture-book register for adults. NEVER comic, NEVER cartoon, NEVER flat
vector, NEVER photoreal, NEVER neon, NEVER 3D or CGI or Pixar, NEVER glossy plastic. Use the
reference ONLY for the illustration STYLE.
An ISOLATED OBJECT STUDY on a plain flat warm cream field, empty edge to edge, with a soft shadow
beneath.
SUBJECT: one tall stack of loose cream PAPER SHEETS sitting on the floor, seen from slightly above
so both the front edge and the flat TOP of the stack are clearly visible. The entire stack is well
inside the frame with generous empty cream space above it.
It must be obviously PAPER and never a solid block: every individual sheet edge is drawn as its own
fine line up the whole height, hundreds of separate leaves, with the tiny natural unevenness of real
paper along the sides, a few sheets sitting a hair proud of their neighbours. Soft, light, flexible
paper, not wood, not stone, not a plank, not a beam, not a carved column.
The work is good: the sheets are crisp, clean and square, the corners sharp, the stack neatly
squared like a well-kept ream.
Not one sheet has ever been taken. No gap anywhere in the column, no sheet pulled part way out, no
leaning, no slump.
Lying openly across the flat top of the stack, and gathered in a clear soft ring on the floor all
around its base, is a visible layer of pale grey DUST, thick enough to read plainly as dust rather
than shadow. A long time has passed and nobody has touched it.
The top of the stack is completely bare. Nothing rests on it. Nothing is built from it.
Calm, still, immaculate, quiet. Not messy, not toppling, not ruined, not garbage. No people, no
figures, no hands. No landscape, no horizon, no sky. No text, no letters, no numbers, no writing on
any sheet.
READABLE SURFACES FACE THEIR READER: any book, page, letter, scroll or sign a character is reading
or writing faces THAT CHARACTER, never the camera. Compose over-the-shoulder or from behind so the
surface genuinely faces them. Any handwriting is abstract handwriting-like line work with NO real
readable letters.
NO USER INTERFACE ANYWHERE: no windows, menu bars, buttons, icons, toolbars, panels, form fields,
cursors, floating rectangles or screenshot-like elements. Screens carry only soft glow or vague
painterly shapes.

Invariants the read-back must check:
1. It must be unmistakably a stack of PAGES: individual sheet edges legible all the way up, with the slight unevenness real paper has. A smooth column reads as wood or stone and the metaphor dies. And the work is visibly GOOD: crisp, square, finished, never scruffy or junk
2. The TOP is visible and BARE. The stack is tall, but it terminates, and nothing rests on it and nothing continues from it. Running it out of frame hides that it stops, which is the whole point
3. NOTHING HAS BEEN TAKEN FROM IT. The stack is perfectly aligned with no sheet pulled out, no gap, no leaning, no disturbance of any kind
4. Nothing is built from it and nothing rests on it. It supports no structure and leads nowhere
5. Soft settled dust at its base and along its top edges, so that time has visibly passed with nothing removed
6. No person, no mess, no despair, no chaos, no toppling. It is calm and immaculate, which is what makes it sad
7. Warm editorial register: cream ground, soft painterly line, muted natural palette
8. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, or readable marks on any sheet

## Two failures found on roll 1, both now written into the invariants

**It came back as a wooden post.** A smooth column with faint striations reads as timber or stone, and if the viewer cannot tell it is finished WORK, the metaphor does not exist. The fix is to draw every sheet edge as its own line with the slight unevenness real paper has.

**The dust registered as shadow.** Dust is not atmosphere here, it is the evidence that time passed and nobody took any. It has to be plainly, almost too obviously, dust.

Roll 1 also ran the stack out of the top of the frame, which was my idea and a bad one. A bare visible TOP is the stronger image: the stack terminates, nothing rests on it, nothing continues from it. That is the whole point, and hiding the top hid it.
