# ruth: generation prompts

Register anchor (`reference/style/warm-editorial-neutral/refs/warm-editorial-swatch.png`) is passed FIRST on every shot. Warm editorial ink-and-wash; never neon, 3D/CGI/Pixar, glossy plastic, HUD/arc-reactor comic vocabulary, neo-comic.

Character matrix. REQUIRED is forward-fullbody plus face-neutral, and both are locked, so this reads `partial` rather than `locked`. The remaining six slots (face-3q, expressions, profiles, back, signature-pose) are unshot and honestly null.

## forward-fullbody  -> reference/ruth/forward-fullbody.png

Warm editorial storybook illustration, soft painterly line and gentle shading, muted natural palette
on a warm cream ground. Picture-book register for adults. NEVER comic, NEVER cartoon, NEVER flat
vector, NEVER photoreal, NEVER neon, NEVER 3D or CGI or Pixar, NEVER glossy plastic. Use the
reference ONLY for the illustration STYLE.
A CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEET: one single full-length standing figure, facing the viewer straight on,
feet visible, arms relaxed at her sides, neutral easy posture. Isolated on a plain flat warm cream
field, empty edge to edge, with a soft shadow beneath her feet.
SUBJECT: a Latina woman in her sixties. Dark hair heavily streaked with silver, worn in a short
practical cut. Small reading glasses hang on a fine beaded cord around her neck, resting on her
chest, not on her face. She wears a soft rust-coloured cardigan over a simple cream blouse, plain
dark trousers, flat comfortable shoes. Her expression is warm and unhurried, gently attentive,
neither stern nor sugary.
She looks like a capable, practical woman who has taught children for a long time and is comfortable
in a room.
Nothing denominational and nothing costume-like: no habit, no veil, no clerical collar, no cross or
religious jewellery, no robe. No props in her hands. No background, no furniture, no room. No other
people. No text, no letters, no numbers, no words anywhere.
READABLE SURFACES FACE THEIR READER: any book, page, letter, scroll or sign a character is reading
or writing faces THAT CHARACTER, never the camera. Compose over-the-shoulder or from behind so the
surface genuinely faces them. Any handwriting is abstract handwriting-like line work with NO real
readable letters.
NO USER INTERFACE ANYWHERE: no windows, menu bars, buttons, icons, toolbars, panels, form fields,
cursors, floating rectangles or screenshot-like elements. Screens carry only soft glow or vague
painterly shapes.
