# spatial-lenses — reference matrix prompts

Prop, SPEC §12: hero + detail (+ worn). Shoot with `--register warm-editorial-neutral`
so the pack's anchor is passed FIRST.

Register negatives (bake on every shot): neon, 3D/CGI/Pixar, glossy plastic,
HUD/arc-reactor comic vocabulary, neo-comic, photoreal.

Load-bearing detail (what `detail` locks): the slim old-gold inlay running along
the temple and brow of a CLEAR champagne acetate frame, and the faint smoke-gold
lens tint that never hides the eye behind it.

> **Do NOT pass `lineage/hero.png` or `lineage/detail.png` as references.** They are
> the sniped design record from the christofuturism universe and they are photoreal,
> which is a rejected pole here. Shoot from the invariants, not from those plates.

## hero -> `reference/spatial-lenses/hero.png` (1024x1024)

Warm editorial ink-and-wash illustration in the style of the reference image. One
pair of eyeglasses resting folded on a plain warm cream ground and filling most of
the frame, seen from a low three-quarter angle: an elegant modern wayfarer shape in
CLEAR translucent champagne acetate, with a slim old-gold inlay running along the
temples and the brow line. The lenses carry only a faint smoke-gold tint and stay
clear enough to see through. They are ordinary handsome eyeglasses, NEVER goggles,
NEVER a visor, NEVER a headset, NEVER opaque sunglasses, and NEVER black frames. No
logo, no brand mark, no lettering anywhere. Soft warm daylight, gentle paper
texture, quiet shadows.

## detail -> `reference/spatial-lenses/detail.png` (1024x1024)

Warm editorial ink-and-wash illustration in the style of the reference image. A
tight close-up crop of the same eyeglasses at the hinge and brow: the clear
translucent champagne acetate frame filling the frame, the slim old-gold inlay
running along the temple and across the brow line, and one lens carrying only a
faint smoke-gold tint. Absolutely no logo, no brand mark, no lettering, no screws
rendered as branding. Soft warm light raking across the acetate so its clarity and
the gold line both read.

## worn -> `reference/spatial-lenses/worn.png` (1024x1024)

Warm editorial ink-and-wash illustration in the style of the FIRST reference image,
matching the eyeglasses reference exactly. A calm three-quarter portrait of an
ordinary woman in her forties, South Asian, dark hair pinned back, in a plain sage
blouse, wearing the same clear champagne acetate glasses with the slim old-gold
inlay. HER EYES ARE CLEARLY VISIBLE through the faintly smoke-gold lenses and she
is looking slightly off camera, relaxed. The gold inlay carries a faint warm glow
along its line and nothing else: NO floating panels, NO projected images, NO
readable text in the air, NO lit screen, NO HUD. Plain warm cream background. No
lettering anywhere. Never photoreal.
