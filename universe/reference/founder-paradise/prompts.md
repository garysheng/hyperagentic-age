# founder-heaven — generation prompts

Register anchor (`reference/style/warm-editorial-neutral/refs/warm-editorial-swatch.png`) is passed FIRST as the style anchor on every shot. warm editorial ink-and-wash; never neon, 3D/CGI/Pixar, glossy plastic, HUD/arc-reactor comic vocabulary, neo-comic, photoreal.

The `establishing` plate is the identity of record for the island's silhouette; shoot it first and pass it on the `terrace` shot so both plates agree on the architecture vocabulary (pale terraces, cream buildings, brass wind-spires).

**Negatives (every shot):**
- people or figures of any kind (empty plates)
- text, signage or lettering anywhere
- chains, pillars, engines, thrusters, rockets holding the island up
- chrome, neon, glass skyscrapers, sci-fi HUD vocabulary
- photoreal rendering
- 3D/CGI gloss

## establishing (1536x1024) -> reference/founder-heaven/establishing.png
An EMPTY establishing plate, nobody in it: a floating protopian island high in warm open sky, seen from below and afar, above a jungle canopy that fills only the lower quarter of the frame with soft emerald treetops. The island's underside is a gentle teardrop of pale rock trailing thin skirts of mist. Its top carries terraced gardens stepping up from the rim, low cream buildings with warm windows, small orchards and pools, and a few slender brass wind-spires catching the light. The island reads about the size of a small hill town, far enough away that no individual door can be read; a few birds near it read as specks. Small soft clouds drift beside it. It floats free: nothing holds it up. Graceful and quiet, never chrome, never neon. Soft ink-and-wash illustration with visible brushwork and watercolor paper texture in sky, rock and leaves alike. No people, no text.

A low stone rail runs along the rim, and beyond it open sky drops away to the jungle far below, reading as a soft green sea with no individual trees. Behind the terrace, terraced gardens step up to low cream buildings with warm windows, and one slender brass wind-spire rises against the sky. Planting spills over the terrace edges; a small pool catches the light. The architecture matches the attached establishing plate exactly: same pale stone, same cream buildings, same brass spires.
