# the-portal — generation prompts

Register anchor (`reference/style/warm-editorial-neutral/refs/warm-editorial-swatch.png`) is passed FIRST as the style anchor on every shot. warm editorial ink-and-wash; never neon, 3D/CGI/Pixar, glossy plastic, HUD/arc-reactor comic vocabulary, neo-comic, photoreal.

The chain is BLUEPRINT-SEEDED: `blueprint.png` is code-drawn by `abu elevation` from `canon/blueprints/the-portal.elevation.json` (deterministic, free) and rides as conditioning on every shot, so all states share one geometry. `master` is the paint seed and the identity of record; `hidden`, `waning` and `closed` are STATES shot STAR (each off master, never off a sibling), because a reference image outranks any negative and one state's veiling or darkness must not walk into another.

### WHAT MUST NOT DRIFT

One upright pointed-oval doorway of pure warm golden light, soft-edged like wet ink-wash with a thin brighter rim, a few small gold motes drifting near it, its lower tip floating a hand's width above the forest floor. It is light and nothing else: no frame, no ring, no arch, no standing stones, no machinery, no doorsill. Through the opening, warm light and open sky, never darkness, never a void. This era's portal at its opening is 3.8 m tall by 2.0 m wide, about twice a person's height. The same pointed-oval shape at every size; only size, veiling and light change between states.

**Negatives (every shot):**
- a frame, ring, arch, torii, standing stones, machine or doorsill around the light
- hard vector edges, lens flare, god rays, neon or sci-fi glow
- a swirling vortex, spiral or whirlpool of energy
- darkness, a void or stars inside the opening
- the floating island visible anywhere (its silhouette belongs to founder-paradise's own plates)
- any human figure
- lettering, signage or numbers anywhere
- ominous mood or horror gloom
- photoreal rendering
- 3D/CGI gloss
