# the-plain-door — generation prompts

Register: shot in the `warm-editorial-neutral` Style Pack (anchor `reference/style/warm-editorial-neutral/refs/warm-editorial-swatch.png`, passed FIRST on every shot). warm editorial ink-and-wash; never neon, 3D/CGI/Pixar, glossy plastic, HUD/arc-reactor comic vocabulary, neo-comic, photoreal.

The chain is BLUEPRINT-SEEDED: `blueprint.png` is code-drawn by `abu elevation` (deterministic, free) and rides as conditioning on every shot, so all states share one geometry. `master` is the paint seed and the identity of record; `ajar` and `museum` are STATES that differ in light and dressing, so shoot them STAR (each off master, never off a sibling).

**Negatives (every shot):**
- lettering of any kind on the door face
- a sign or plaque mounted on the door
- text or writing anywhere in frame
- any room or scene visible through a door gap
- darkness or storm light through a gap
- ominous mood
- windows or glass panes in the door
- panel count other than four
- knob on the right side of the leaf
- photoreal rendering
- neon light
