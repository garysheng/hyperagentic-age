# the-floating-orb — reference matrix prompts

Prop, SPEC §12: hero + detail (+ docked). Shoot with `--register warm-editorial-neutral`
so the pack's anchor is passed FIRST.

Register negatives (bake on every shot): neon, 3D/CGI/Pixar, glossy plastic,
HUD/arc-reactor comic vocabulary, neo-comic, photoreal.

Load-bearing detail (what `detail` locks): the translucent warm amber glass and the
one slim brass equatorial band, with NO lens, aperture, iris or indicator anywhere
on the surface. The absence is the design.

## hero -> `reference/the-floating-orb/hero.png` (1024x1024)

Warm editorial ink-and-wash illustration in the style of the reference image. One
palm-sized sphere of translucent warm amber glass floating unsupported in a plain
warm cream room, filling most of the frame, with a single slim brass band around
its equator and a soft shadow on the surface below it. It glows gently from within.
It has NO rotors, NO propellers, NO wings, NO stand, NO wire, NO visible camera
lens, NO aperture, NO buttons, NO antennas and NO indicator lights: the surface is
smooth amber glass and the brass band, and nothing else. No logo, no lettering
anywhere. Soft warm daylight, gentle paper texture.

## detail -> `reference/the-floating-orb/detail.png` (1024x1024)

Warm editorial ink-and-wash illustration in the style of the reference image. A
tight close-up crop of the same sphere where the slim brass band meets the
translucent warm amber glass, the inner glow visible through the depth of the
glass. Absolutely NO lens, NO aperture, NO iris, NO seam that reads as a shutter,
NO indicator light, NO lettering and NO logo anywhere on the surface. Soft warm
light raking across the glass.

## docked -> `reference/the-floating-orb/docked.png` (1024x1024)

Warm editorial ink-and-wash illustration in the style of the FIRST reference image,
matching the sphere reference exactly. The same palm-sized amber glass sphere at
rest, settled into a shallow plain pale-wood dock on a side table by a window, its
inner glow dimmed low as it charges. The dock is a simple turned wooden cradle with
no cables, no connectors, no lights and no markings. The room is quiet and warm and
mostly empty. No lettering anywhere, no logo on the dock or the sphere. Soft
afternoon light. Never photoreal.
