# the-context-castle — reference shoot prompts

Scaffolded 2026-08-15 (abu:add-visual-metaphor). Run by `shoot-references`; art is NOT
generated at authoring time. Status stays `unlocked` until every plate below is shot, read
back against `structured.invariants`, locked via `abu lock-shot`, and blessed by Gary.

**Every shot:** pass the register anchor FIRST:
`reference/style/warm-editorial-neutral/refs/warm-editorial-swatch.png`
(house register: warm editorial ink-and-wash — soft ink linework, warm washes, cream paper light).

**Negatives on every shot (register rejected poles + entity poles):** neon; 3D/CGI/Pixar;
glossy plastic; HUD/arc-reactor comic vocabulary; neo-comic; photoreal; blue/white flat
diagram palette (the HCP website's language — explicitly banned here, the cartridge owns
the palette); siege imagery, armies, storm-threat.

**Invariant across ALL plates (say it in every prompt so the object reads as ONE object):**
a walled castle of warm sand-and-grey stone with rust/clay roof accents; EXACTLY ONE gate in
the curtain wall (honest timber, simple iron fittings, modest and human-doored); distinct
deliberately-designed rooms and low towers inside the wall, lamplit and inhabited; at the
exact center the KEEP, visibly innermost and tallest, smooth pale stone, windowless at the
base, one sealed door with no outside handle. Same castle, same geometry, in every plate.
The massing blueprint fixes LAYOUT ONLY — never copy its bare flat-grey block surfaces:
every room is a FINISHED, deliberately designed little building with its own silhouette
and its own pitched or shaped roof in rust and clay tones, timber doors, warm lamplit
windows; the castle reads as hand-built warm sand-and-grey stone, a cared-for city of one
life, never brutalist bare-concrete massing blocks. (Added 2026-08-15 after the first
master roll came back as painted massing: identical concrete cubes, no roofs, no accents.)
After the master is shot, pass the master as an additional reference on every state plate.
