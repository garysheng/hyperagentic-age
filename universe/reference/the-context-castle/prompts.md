# the-context-castle — reference shoot prompts (HOLOGRAPHIC redesign, 2026-08-15)

Scaffolded 2026-08-15 (abu:add-visual-metaphor); first solid-stone matrix shot, locked, and
SUPERSEDED the same day per Gary on plate review ("Feel like the castle should be
holographic") — see `superseded/2026-08-15-solid-stone/`. This file shoots the holographic
matrix: the castle as a translucent holographic projection rising from the open
supercharged laptop. Run by `shoot-references`; locked via `abu lock-shot` after read-back.

**Every shot:** pass the register anchor FIRST:
`reference/style/warm-editorial-neutral/refs/warm-editorial-swatch.png`
(house register: warm editorial ink-and-wash — soft ink linework, warm washes, cream paper light).

**Negatives on every shot (register rejected poles + entity poles):** neon, 3D/CGI/Pixar, glossy plastic, HUD/arc-reactor comic vocabulary, neo-comic, photoreal, blue/white flat diagram palette, cyan or blue hologram of any kind, solid opaque stone castle, siege imagery, armies, storm-threat.

**Invariant across ALL plates (say it in every prompt so the object reads as ONE object):**
the castle is a TRANSLUCENT HOLOGRAPHIC PROJECTION in this universe's digital vocabulary:
glowing warm clay-coral-to-amber light (kin to the supercharged laptop's orange, ~#D97757
family), slightly see-through, luminous, casting LIGHT not shadow — never solid masonry,
never cyan, never wireframe-sterile. Within the hologram the architecture stays fully
DESIGNED: a walled castle whose curtain wall has EXACTLY ONE gate (timber-grain, simple
fittings, modest and human-doored); distinct deliberately-designed rooms and low towers
inside the wall, each with its own silhouette and its own pitched or shaped roof reading
as luminous rust-and-clay tints; brighter warm motes where lamplit windows would be, so it
reads inhabited and loved; at the exact center the KEEP, visibly innermost and tallest,
the quietest and LEAST TRANSPARENT light in the frame, windowless at the base, one sealed
door with no outside handle. Same castle, same geometry, in every plate. The massing
blueprint fixes LAYOUT ONLY — never copy its bare flat-grey block surfaces: take from it
only the circular curtain wall, its single gate opening, the ring of room footprints, and
the keep at the exact center. In every frame wide enough to show the castle whole, the
hologram rises from the lit screen of an OPEN supercharged laptop on a real wooden desk:
the laptop per its own locked law (unmistakable clamshell, visible physical keyboard,
screen face visible to the viewer, the WHOLE laptop glowing translucent orange, casting
light not shadow), while the desk and the warm room around it stay PHYSICAL — solid,
warm gold-lit, casting ordinary shadows. The hologram casts a soft warm pool of light
down over the keys.

## master  →  `reference/the-context-castle/master.png` (1536x1024)

REFS: supercharged-laptop@orange+scale

The canonical view. A real wooden desk in a warm, physical room, late-afternoon light. On
the desk an OPEN supercharged laptop — an unmistakable clamshell with a visible physical
keyboard, its whole body glowing translucent warm orange, screen face visible, casting
light not shadow. Rising from the lit screen, filling the upper two-thirds of the frame, a
TRANSLUCENT HOLOGRAPHIC CASTLE glowing warm clay-coral-to-amber: seen from a slight
three-quarter elevation so the one gate, the ring of designed rooms, and the innermost
sealed keep are all legible in a single frame. The hologram is slightly see-through — the
warm room reads faintly through its edges — and casts a soft warm pool of light over the
keys and desk. Nothing entering or leaving; no figures; no beam. The castle reads whole,
sovereign, and loved. The desk and room are solid and physical with ordinary soft shadows.
No lettering anywhere in this plate.

## at-rest  →  `reference/the-context-castle/at-rest.png` (1536x1024)

REFS: supercharged-laptop@orange+scale

ARGUED STATE: the castle at rest, whole and sovereign. Same geometry and same holographic
treatment as the master: the translucent warm coral-amber hologram rising from the open
supercharged laptop on its physical desk, in a calm dusk-lit room. Every window-mote in
the hologram warm and bright. The one gate is fitted with honest TIMBER DOORS drawn in
the hologram's luminous linework, and they are visibly SHUT, closed flush across the
whole archway — NEVER an open archway, NEVER a dark empty passage, NEVER a doorless
opening (added 2026-08-15 after roll 1 came back with an open dark arch). Nothing
crosses the hologram's edge anywhere. The argument of this plate is completeness: nothing is missing, nothing is
leaking, nobody is waiting at the wall. No figures, no beam, no slivers. The room around
the laptop is dim, warm, and physical; the hologram is the brightest thing in it, casting
light not shadow.

## gate-sliver  →  `reference/the-context-castle/gate-sliver.png` (1536x1024)

REFS: supercharged-laptop@orange+scale

ARGUED STATE: the gate opening as a single sliver is passed out. The same translucent
holographic castle above its open supercharged laptop; the camera closer in over the
laptop, level with the hologram's one timber-grain gate. The gate's timber doors stand
ajar exactly wide enough for a hand-off. At the threshold, floating at door-handle height
in the narrow gap, ONE SMALL pale-gold tile the size of a playing card — TINY next to the
gate, no bigger than one stone of the wall — is being passed out of the hologram. It
carries exactly ONE short legible English word as its label: TASTE. It is clearly a
fragment of something larger, and it floats free, touching nothing. NEVER a plank, ramp,
walkway, drawbridge, slab, or bridge lying through the gateway; NEVER anything that
spans the opening or rests on the ground; NEVER gibberish glyphs (added 2026-08-15
after roll 1 came back as a gate-wide plank ramp covered in pseudo-text). The curtain
wall shows no other opening of any kind. Shoot the EMPTY-PLATE version for the
contract: the gate ajar and the sliver mid-pass with no figure, so the casting
(chief-of-agents as gatekeeper) binds at spread time from its own locked sheets rather
than being baked into this plate. The sliver is small; nothing else leaves. Laptop, desk,
and room physical beneath, per the master.

## beam-to-service  →  `reference/the-context-castle/beam-to-service.png` (1536x1024)

REFS: supercharged-laptop@orange+scale

ARGUED STATE: a thin beam carrying the sliver to a distant service, leaving ONLY through
the one gate. Wide landscape frame: on one side, the open supercharged laptop on its
physical desk with the large, detailed holographic castle glowing above it; far away on
the other side, small and at or beyond the room's horizon (a far windowsill, the dim end
of a long desk), a tiny generic unbranded service — a small plain hologram-tile or modest
generic building silhouette, no logo, no recognizable architecture. From the castle's ONE
GATE — and from nowhere else on the wall, never from a tower, never from the keep — a
single THIN taut thread of warm gold light crosses the distance, the labeled sliver a
bright fleck traveling along it. The beam is thinner than any wall it passes — a thread,
never a flood, never a pipe. The castle visibly outweighs the service in size and detail,
because the person outweighs the vendor.

## keep-sealed  →  `reference/the-context-castle/keep-sealed.png` (1536x1024)

ARGUED STATE: the keep at the center sealed shut — the inner life that never leaves, under
any incentive. CAMERA, BINDING (kept from the solid-stone shoot, where roll 1 came back as
another whole-castle wide shot): this is a CLOSE-UP, not a vista. The camera is INSIDE the
hologram, just above the luminous rooftops, looking at the keep; the keep and its sealed
door FILL most of the frame. The curtain wall, the gate, the laptop, the desk, and the
room are all OUT OF FRAME. Only the keep, its sealed handle-less door, and the nearest
softened holographic rooftops are visible. NOT a wide shot, NOT an aerial view, NO gate
anywhere in frame, NO laptop in frame. The medium stays fully holographic: everything in
frame is translucent glowing warm coral-amber light, the keep the quietest and LEAST
transparent form, smooth and windowless at its base, its one shut door with NO handle on
the outside. No beam, no sliver, because in this state nothing is leaving. Quiet, warm,
resolved — sealed reads as peace and sovereignty, never as menace or imprisonment. No
lettering in this plate.
