# the-orange-compass — reference matrix prompts

Prop, SPEC §12: hero + detail. Shoot with `shoot-references` (chain_matrix.py). The
universe declares both an inline register anchor and `identity.register.stylePack`
(the same warm-editorial-neutral swatch family); shoot with
`--register warm-editorial-neutral` so the pack's anchor is passed FIRST.

Register negatives (bake on every shot): neon, 3D/CGI/Pixar, glossy plastic,
HUD/arc-reactor comic vocabulary, neo-comic, photoreal.

Load-bearing detail (what `detail` locks): the plain warm-cream face with NO
letters, numbers or degree marks, and the single dark needle tipped in the same
clay orange as the shell.

Warm editorial ink-and-wash illustration in the style of the reference image. One
small round pocket compass, palm-sized, resting on a plain warm cream ground and
filling most of the frame: a burnt-clay orange enamel shell with a thin brass rim,
hung on a short braided cream cord that trails to one side. Under its glass, a
plain warm cream face with a single dark needle tipped in the same clay orange.
NO letters, numbers, degree marks, logos or brand marks anywhere on it. The orange
is matte and warm, in the same clay family as rust cloth, never neon and never
safety-vest orange. Soft warm daylight, gentle paper texture, quiet shadows.
