# church-classroom: generation prompts

Register anchor (`reference/style/warm-editorial-neutral/refs/warm-editorial-swatch.png`) is passed FIRST on every shot. Warm editorial ink-and-wash; never neon, 3D/CGI/Pixar, glossy plastic, HUD/arc-reactor comic vocabulary, neo-comic.

A setting is contract-shaped, not matrix-shaped: turnaround, emptyPlates, blueprint plus the map/blocking/dressing/scale prose. The establishing plate IS the turnaround and the only empty plate; the blueprint chains off it so the plan and the room agree.

## establishing  -> reference/church-classroom/establishing.png

Warm editorial storybook illustration, soft painterly line and gentle shading, muted natural palette
on a warm cream ground. Picture-book register for adults. NEVER comic, NEVER cartoon, NEVER flat
vector, NEVER photoreal, NEVER neon, NEVER 3D or CGI or Pixar, NEVER glossy plastic. Use the
reference ONLY for the illustration STYLE.
An EMPTY ESTABLISHING PLATE of one small room. No people at all: the room is unoccupied and waiting.
A modest little room in a small church, used for reading to children. A worn soft rug covers the
middle of the wooden floor. Six low child-sized wooden chairs stand in a loose half-circle on the
rug, facing one slightly larger plain reading chair. Along the left wall runs a low open shelf
holding picture books stood upright, their spines completely blank and unmarked. One tall window on
the right wall lets warm afternoon daylight fall in a soft slant across the rug. Cream painted
walls, a little scuffed near the floor. Two floor cushions lie beside the rug.
Low camera, near a small child's eye height, looking across the rug toward the reading chair, the
window light coming from the right.
Warm, modest, quiet, clearly loved and a little worn. Ordinary and domestic.
This is NOT a sanctuary: no pews, no altar, no pulpit, no stage, no stained glass, no cross, no
religious statues or imagery of any kind, nothing denominational. No other rooms, no doorways to
elsewhere, no corridor.
No people, no children, no adults, no figures, no hands. No signage, no posters, no banners, no
charts, no labels. No text, no letters, no numbers, no words, no writing anywhere, including on
every single book spine.
READABLE SURFACES FACE THEIR READER: any book, page, letter, scroll or sign a character is reading
or writing faces THAT CHARACTER, never the camera. Compose over-the-shoulder or from behind so the
surface genuinely faces them. Any handwriting is abstract handwriting-like line work with NO real
readable letters.
NO USER INTERFACE ANYWHERE: no windows, menu bars, buttons, icons, toolbars, panels, form fields,
cursors, floating rectangles or screenshot-like elements. Screens carry only soft glow or vague
painterly shapes.

A simple hand-drawn OVERHEAD FLOOR PLAN of that same small room, looking straight down, drawn as a
warm painterly diagram on plain cream rather than a technical blueprint.
The room is a plain rectangle. Along the left wall, the long low bookshelf drawn as a simple bar.
Centred in the room, the round worn rug. On the rug, six small squares in a loose half-circle for
the child chairs, and one slightly larger square for the reading chair at the open end of the half-
circle. On the right wall, a wide gap for the tall window. Two small rounded shapes beside the rug
for the floor cushions. A single door opening on the lower wall.
Soft warm lines, gentle wash fills, slightly wobbly and hand-made rather than mechanical. Simple and
readable at a glance.
